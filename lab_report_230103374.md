## Phase 1

### 3.1 Experimental objective
Modern multi-core CPUs enforce cache coherence (MESI/MOESI) at cache-line granularity (64 B on x86-64). If several processes write to adjacent 8-byte words located in the same cache line, every write invalidates that line in the other cores' caches (false sharing). The experiment maps one `SharedMemory` block into p processes; process t updates slot `t * stride` (uint64). By varying `stride` from 1 to 32 words (8–256 B) the cache-line width is detected empirically, and the overhead is diagnosed with the Karp-Flatt metric:

e(p) = (1/S_p − 1/p) / (1 − 1/p), p ≥ 2,  S_p = T_1/T_p,  E_p = S_p/p.

Expected for Ryzen 7 6800H (64 B line): at p = 8 the time drops between stride 4 (32 B) and stride 8 (64 B).

Total work: M = 2.4·10^7 read-modify-write steps (M/p per worker). Benchmark: `run_benchmark(warmup_runs=2, timed_runs=7)`.
